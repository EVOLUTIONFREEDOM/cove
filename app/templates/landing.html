Intro

Who it is for

You already have an Alpaca account (paper or live). You want a simple phone desk instead of hopping between screens. If you do not have Alpaca, open an account with them first. Cove cannot hold cash for you.

What you do in the app

Create a Cove login (email and password). Link your Alpaca keys in Account. Home shows equity and positions. Trade and Options send orders to Alpaca after you confirm. Watchlist, P&L calendar, dividends, activity, and alerts are reports and reminders — they do not move money on their own.

What it is not

Not a broker. Not a bank. Not tax or investment advice. Not affiliated with Alpaca Markets. Charts and option numbers can be late or wrong. Paper is practice. Live is real. Options can expire worthless.

How to start

1. Install Alpaca Cove from Google Play or the App Store when it is listed.

2. Create your Cove login. New accounts get {{ trial_days }} days free.

3. In Account, paste your Alpaca key and secret (start with Paper if you want practice money).

4. Open Home. If it fills in, the link worked. Then trade only after you have read the order back.
